Questions we hear often

How early should I book gorilla permits?

Permits are limited and sell out in busy months, so book as early as you can. Send your dates and we check what is available.

When is the best time to visit?

Uganda can be visited all year. Many travellers prefer the drier months, which are generally June to September and December to February. We can advise for your plans.

What does the price include?

Each quote lists what is included (transport, lodging, permits, meals) so you can compare clearly.

Can you plan a trip that includes Rwanda?

Yes. We organise combined Uganda and Rwanda trips, including border crossings and transfers.

How do I pay?

You confirm the itinerary first. Payment details are shared with the final quote.
